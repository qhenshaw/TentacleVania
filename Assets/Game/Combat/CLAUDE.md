# Combat

Shared damage model (`TentacleVania.Combat`) used by the creature, enemies, hazards and abilities.

## Damage pipeline

- A hit is a `DamageInfo`: amount, stagger, `ArmorDamageScale` (Corrode, Crusher Mass), `DamageType` (Physical, Electric, Fire, Acid, Radiation), the source `Team`, `HitFlags` (Heavy, Finisher, DashAttack, Hazard), the attacker's `Health` if any, point and direction. Build it with `DamageInfo.Create` and deliver it with `Health.TakeDamage`.
- `DamagePipeline.Resolve` (pure, unit tested) runs: team check, the attacker's `Outgoing` modifiers, the target's `Incoming` modifiers, the assist sliders (`GameSettings.DamageDealtScale` for creature hits, `DamageTakenScale` for hits on the creature), then armor, then low-health protection (`Health.SetProtection(below, scale)`: a hit that would leave health under `below` × max is scaled by `scale`; enemies use it under the grab threshold). Every multiplier stacks multiplicatively, as the design's bonus stacking rule says.
- Teams: a team never damages itself, which covers both "the creature's own acid and fire never hurt it" and "no infighting". `Team.Environment` hurts everyone; tag level hazards with it.
- Armor (Balance Sheet): health damage is cut by up to 30% at full armor, scaling with the armor left; armor takes `Amount × ArmorDamageScale`.
- Modifiers implement `IDamageModifier` (`Order`, `Modify(ref DamageContext)`): scale `HealthScale`, `ArmorScale`, `StaggerScale`, or set `Cancelled` (i-frames). Add them to `Health.Incoming` or `Health.Outgoing` and remove them when the effect ends; `ScaleModifier` covers plain multipliers (consume armor −50%, stagger window +50%, typed resistances via `FilterByType`).
- `HitFlags.Ability` marks ability damage (the Bio-Shield burst): the creature's `OutgoingStatModifier` scales those hits by Cunning's `AbilityDamage` and every other creature hit by Brutality's damage and stagger; set it on new ability hits.
- Status effects: a hit can carry `DamageInfo.Status` (`StatusKind` Slow or Root), `StatusMagnitude` (Slow: speed lost, 0.2 = 20%) and `StatusDuration`. `Status/StatusEffects` (on the creature prefab; add it to anything that can be slowed or rooted) applies it when the hit lands, through `Status/StatusTimers` (pure, unit tested): re-applying refreshes the duration up to its own length and keeps the strongest magnitude, so nothing stacks forever; death clears everything. It glows through `CharacterMaterials.SetStatus` (root over slow; colours on the component) and raises `Started`/`Ended`. `CreatureMotor` multiplies walking by `MoveScale` (0 while rooted) and refuses dashes while rooted; `CreatureCharge` restores the full-charge glow when a status ends.
- Bleed and Burn (TV-138; Balance Sheet): damage over time through `Status/DamageOverTime` (pure, unit tested), tuned in `Resources/StatusEffectSettings.asset` (loaded by `StatusEffectSettings.Instance`). Bleed: 3 dmg/s per stack, max 5 stacks, 5 s; every application adds a stack and refreshes the duration (all stacks expire together; the sheet leaves this open, so it is a first-pass choice). Burn: 5 dmg/s, 4 s, refreshes without stacking. A hit's `StatusMagnitude` is the damage per second per stack (0 = the asset's), `StatusDuration` likewise; the creature's hits scale it by Cunning's `Stats.StatusPotency`. Ticks every 0.5 s as `HitFlags.DamageOverTime` hits (Physical / Fire) from the applying team with no `Attacker`, so they skip outgoing modifiers, charge gain, hit feedback, hit effects and dash i-frames, but still go through the target's incoming modifiers, armor and low-health protection. `BurstBleed(scale)` deals the stacks' remaining damage at once and raises `BleedBurst` (the Hemorrhage hook). Each `StatusEffects` plays the asset's `ApplySound` (`event:/Combat/Status/BleedApply`, `BurnIgnite`) and a looping child VFX (`VFX/Status/BleedDrip`, `BurnFlames` placeholders) while active. `Enemy` adds a `StatusEffects` at `Awake` with `Glow` off (enemies' status glow belongs to `TelegraphGlow`); the creature glows burn over bleed under root and slow. The component disables itself while nothing is active, so idle targets cost no `Update`. F5 Combat: Bleed / Burn nearest enemy, Burst nearest enemy's bleed, Nearest enemy status, Bleed / Burn creature, Clear creature statuses.
- Static and chain lightning (TV-139; Balance Sheet: ~5 electric hits on a soldier, 30 dmg to up to 3 nearby enemies, then reset): `StaticMeter` and `ChainRules.PickNearest` (pure, unit tested). Every applied `DamageType.Electric` hit from `Team.Creature` adds `StaticPerHit` (20 of `StaticCapacity` 100, the same for every enemy: a first-pass reading of "~5 hits"), scaled by `StatusPotency` and the static `StatusEffects.StaticBuildScale` (Arc Conductor); a hit with `Status = Static` adds its `StatusMagnitude` instead. The creature never builds static. At full, the meter resets and chain lightning hits the nearest `ChainTargets` (+ `ExtraChainTargets`) living members of the target's team within `ChainRadius` 6 m (not the source unless `ChainHitsSource`) for `ChainDamage` × `ChainDamageScale` Electric with `HitFlags.ChainLightning` and no `Attacker` (no charge, feedback or static of its own); `ChainAddsStatic` (Overload Chain) adds static to each target, cascading up to 8 deep. `FillStatic` (Shock tier 3) and `ReleaseStatic(scale)` (Grounding Spike; damage × the fill) are the hooks, `StaticDischarged` the event. Every `StatusEffects` is in a static registry for the target search. Placeholder visuals: `ChainLightningLines` (a scene-root pool of 12 jittered `LineRenderer`s on `TurretLaser.mat`, prewarmed by the first `StatusEffects`), `event:/Combat/Status/ChainLightning`, and a cyan static bar on enemy bars. F5 Combat: Static hit / Fill / Release nearest enemy, Chain lightning discharges.
- Corrode and acid pools (TV-140; Balance Sheet): Corrode is a `StatusTimers` status (4 s, refreshes, never stacks) that adds a `ScaleModifier` to the target's `Incoming` while active: health damage ×1.2 and armor damage × `CorrodeArmorDamage` 1.5 (the sheet says TBD, so placeholder). Apply it with `Status = Corrode` on a hit or `StatusEffects.Apply`; looping `VFX/Status/CorrodeDrip`, `event:/Combat/Status/CorrodeApply`, green glow on the creature. `AcidPools.Spawn(position, team[, radius, duration, damagePerSecond])` places a pooled acid pool (scene-root `AcidPools`, `AcidPoolCount` 12 slots in `AcidPoolSlots`, pure and unit tested; when full the one ending soonest is reused): a `DecalProjector` with its own copy of `Materials/AcidPoolDecal.mat` (the `TelegraphDecal` shader, filled green circle), parked rather than disabled when idle. It lasts 4 s (sheet), fades over its last 0.4 s and, on landing and every 0.5 s, hits every hurtbox (Enemy and Creature layers) whose centre stands inside its radius and that its team can damage: `AcidPoolDamagePerSecond` × 0.5 Acid as a `DamageOverTime` hit plus Corrode. Radius 1.5 m and 6 dmg/s are placeholders; the creature's own pools never hurt it (team rule). `event:/Combat/Status/AcidPoolSplash` on landing. F5 Combat: Corrode nearest enemy / creature, Acid pool under nearest enemy, Hostile acid pool under creature, Acid pools active.
- Parasites (TV-141; no Balance Sheet numbers yet, all placeholders in the settings asset): `StatusKind.Parasite` is a refreshing `DamageOverTime` (2 dmg/s, 8 s) with a looping `VFX/Status/ParasiteWrithe` and `event:/Combat/Status/ParasiteApply`. An infected target that dies bursts (`ParasitesBurstOnDeath`, default on; `ParasiteBurstScale`; `ParasiteBurst` event): 15 damage and infection to every living member of its team within 3 m, chaining through the same cascade guard as chain lightning. `SpreadParasites(radius, team)` infects without damage (Host Feeding). `ParasiteSwarm.Spawn(position, count, team)` (scene-root pool of `MinionCount` 12 `Prefabs/ParasiteMinion` primitives in `MinionSlots`, pure and unit tested; the oldest is reused when full) runs minions that walk straight at `StatusEffects.NearestHostile` within 10 m (no NavMesh: they live 6 s) and bite for 4 every 0.6 s, each bite infecting (`event:/Combat/Status/MinionBite`); it also plays the pooled `ParasiteBurst` VFX. F5 Combat: Infect nearest enemy, Spread parasites, Spawn 2 parasite minions, Parasites burst on death, Parasite bursts / minions.
- `TakeDamage` returns a `DamageResult` (`Applied`, `Ignored` by team or death, `Blocked` by a modifier) with the health, armor and stagger dealt. Stagger isn't stored here: the stagger system reads `StaggerDamage` from the `Damaged` event.
- Events: `Health.Damaged`, `Healed`, `Died` per target, and the global `CombatEvents.HitLanded` for every applied hit (charge meter gain, hit feedback, lifesteal). `CombatEvents.ConsumeStarted` fires when a consume grabs its victim (`Consumable.BeginConsume`).
- `IStatsSource` marks a component that sets its object's `Health` (and `Consumable`) at runtime (`Enemy` from its `EnemyDefinition`, `CreatureVitals` from `HatchlingVitals`); `Editor/DrivenStatsEditor` then shows those fields read-only with a note naming the asset, so nobody tunes the prefab copy.

## Hit feedback

- `Feedback/HitFeedback` (on the creature prefab, settings `Data/HitFeedback.asset`) listens to `CombatEvents.HitLanded` and `CombatEvents.StaggerBroken` (raised by `Enemy` when a bar breaks) and, once per frame for the strongest event, requests hit-stop, screen shake and rumble from `Core.Feedback`. `HitFeedbackRules.Classify` (unit tested) maps a hit to Light, Heavy, Finisher, DashAttack (the player's hits, by flag), CreatureHurt (hits on the player) or None (hazards, enemy-on-enemy); StaggerBreak outranks everything.
- Each `HitFeedbackLevel` also carries the enemy reaction: `Knockback` metres (over `KnockbackDuration`) and `Flinch` degrees (over `FlinchDuration`), read by `Enemy` through `HitFeedback.Settings`. The defaults are first-pass feel values, not from the design: tune them in the asset. Debug: Combat tab "Last hit feedback", "Hit-stop" and "Rumble" toggles.
- `Feedback/HitEffects` (on the creature prefab, settings `Data/HitEffects.asset`; developer decisions) plays hit VFX and blood decals from `CombatEvents.HitLanded`, picked by `HitEffectRules` (unit tested): impact on every applied hit, blood spray when it deals health damage, a floor decal on kills and big hits (enemy: Heavy, Finisher or DashAttack class; creature: at least `_creatureBigHit` of max health). Death gore (TV-76, developer decisions; `DeathGoreRules`, unit tested): `Health.Die` raises `CombatEvents.Died`; a target with `Health.BurstsOnDeath` (every non-boss enemy) that isn't being consumed bursts: a spray, a decal under the body and `_killGibs` pooled Rigidbody gibs (`GibPool`, `VFX/Gore/FleshGib`, every `_boneEvery`th one `BoneGib`; machines `MetalGib` with the `Machine` side: `MachineSparks` and the `OilDecal`), and its killing hit plays only the impact. Gibs are on layer TransparentFX (so decal raycasts miss them) and exclude the Creature and Enemy layers, and they shrink away after `_gibLifetime`. `CombatEvents.ConsumeBitten` (when the maw closes) does the same burst with `_biteGibs`; `Consumable.CoreBleeds` (bosses) makes machine bosses bleed. Gore off: no gibs, sprays or decals; kills play `IchorSmoke` and `Enemy` sinks the body over its last 0.8 s, and bites play nothing. With gore on `Enemy` hides its renderers on death. Hits on non-bleeding targets play the `Machine` impact. Creature or enemy side comes from `CombatTargets.Creature`. `GameSettings.Gore` off, or a target whose `Health.Bleeds` is false (machines from `EnemyDefinition.Machine`, security relays), skips spray and decals. Everything is prewarmed at `Awake` under a scene-root `HitEffects` object: a ring of `PoolSize` copies per VFX prefab (moved to the hit point, local +Z along the hit direction, exposed `Intensity` set, `Reinit` + `Play`) and `DecalCap` + 1 `DecalProjector`s, where the oldest starts fading out once the cap is reached; the projectors stay enabled and hidden ones are parked at y -1000 with `fadeFactor` 0, since enabling a `DecalProjector` allocates. Decals are raycast onto `_groundLayers` (Default) below the hit point. F5 Combat tab: Gore, Hit effects, decal count, Clear blood decals, Gibs active, Clear gibs (Enemies tab: Kill all).

## Hurtboxes

`Hurtbox` maps a collider to its `Health`; weapon casts resolve hits with `Hurtbox.TryGet(collider, out hurtbox)` (a dictionary, no `GetComponent`). The creature's hurtbox is its `CharacterController`: one body hurtbox, never the tentacles.

## Creature

`Creature/CreatureVitals` adds the dash i-frames as a cancelling modifier and restarts the arena (reloads the creature's scene) 1.5 s after death, the developer's choice for the prototype until nests exist.

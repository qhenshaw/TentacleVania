# Core

Shared code every area may reference (`TentacleVania.Core`). Keep it free of gameplay rules.

## Character master shader

- `Shaders/CharacterMaster.shadergraph` (built from `CharacterMaster.shadergraph.json` + `.hlsl` with `Tools/shadergraph-build.sh`) is the Lit master for every character: `_BaseMap`, `_BaseColor`, `_Smoothness`, `_EmissionColor`, plus effect channels `_GlowColor`/`_GlowAmount`/`_GlowFresnel`/`_GlowFill` (yellow/red attack telegraphs: the same fresnel rim plus fill as status, without the pulse; `SetGlow(color, amount, fresnel, fill)`, tuned per telegraph colour on `TelegraphGlow`), `_FlashColor`/`_FlashAmount` (hit flash) and `_StatusColor`/`_StatusAmount`/`_StatusPulse`/`_StatusFresnel`/`_StatusFill` (stun, charge full, feedable, Bio-Shield, status effects; developer decision: a fresnel rim `pow(1 - N·V, _StatusFresnel)` (default 3; 0 falls back to 3) whose brightness pulses at `_StatusPulse` Hz when > 0, plus a steady `_StatusFill` (default 0.15) on camera-facing surfaces).
- `Shaders/EnvironmentMaster.shadergraph` (Lit, alpha clip) is the master for walls and props: `_BaseMap`, `_BaseColor`, `_NormalMap`, `_Smoothness`, `_EmissionColor` and `_Fade`. When `_Fade` > 0 it dithers away (4×4 Bayer, never in the shadow pass) a screen-space hole around the global `_EnvironmentFadeFocus` (viewport xy, radius as a fraction of screen height, aspect). The greybox materials and the security relay and door use it. Third-party kit materials need `Assets/Game` copies on it before they can fade.
- `Rendering/OcclusionFader` (on the `GameplayCamera` prefab) casts 5 rays from the camera to points on the creature (`CameraFocus.Target`) against `_occluders` (Default). Each hit renderer whose materials all have `_Fade` gets material instances (made once, cached), assigned while it fades in or out (up to 0.85, speed 4/s), and gets its shared materials back once clear. It also sets `_EnvironmentFadeFocus`. No per-frame allocations. F5 Camera tab: Wall fade, Faded walls.
- `Characters/CharacterMaterials` sits on each character root, instances the materials of its listed renderers (and of runtime renderers passed to `Register`, e.g. leg and tentacle lines) and is the only thing that sets those properties: `SetGlow`, `Flash` (fades out over its duration, dimmed when `GameSettings.Flashes` is off), `SetStatus`. No `MaterialPropertyBlock`s. `Register(VisualEffect)` forwards `SetStatus`/`ClearStatus` to an effect's exposed `StatusColor`/`StatusAmount`/`StatusPulse` and, when the graph exposes them, `StatusFresnel`/`StatusFill` (the creature's tentacle strips). `SetStatus(color, amount, pulseHz, fresnel, fill)`: each caller has inspector fields for all of them; glow and flash don't reach effects.

## Tentacle merge (rendering)

- `Rendering/TentacleMergeFeature` (first renderer feature on `PC_Renderer`, before SSAO) smooths deferred normals where creature surfaces overlap so the mass shades as one surface: after the GBuffer it copies `_GBuffer2`, blurs the normals of pixels on the `TentacleMerge` rendering layer at half resolution (depth-aware, `_radius` in metres, `_depthFalloff`, `_strength`) and writes them back before lighting. Shading only: silhouettes and shadows don't merge.
- Creature pixels come from the rendering-layer GBuffer target (`R16_UInt`, written because the RP asset has Use Rendering Layers on), not from re-drawing the renderers: re-drawing the dense VFX strips cost ~1 ms at 1080p. Put a renderer in the merge by adding the `TentacleMerge` rendering layer (the creature's body, maw and VFX renderer have it). It needs the deferred path; in forward it does nothing.
- `Rendering/TentacleMerge.shadergraph` is a Fullscreen graph whose `_MergePass` picks downsample, horizontal blur, vertical blur or composite; the pass keeps one material per stage because a material's values are read when the command buffer runs. The uint rendering-layer texture is a plain HLSL global (`_TentacleMergeLayers`), since graph texture properties are float. `GameSettings.TentacleMerge` turns it off (F5 Creature tab).
- `Rendering/LowHealthTendrilsFeature` (last feature on `PC_Renderer`) draws dark tendrils creeping in from the screen edges after post-processing. It copies the camera colour, then composites it through the `LowHealthTendrils` Fullscreen graph (`_Source`, `_Amount`, `_Pulse`, `_Reach` 0.22 of screen height, so the play area stays clear). The pass is skipped unless the static `Amount` > 0 and `Enabled`, so it costs nothing at healthy HP; at full strength the gpu-ab evals measured it within noise (about 0.05 ms) at 1080p. `LowHealthRules` (unit tested) holds the steps (light at 40% HP, heavy at 15%), the heartbeat shape and the consume lurch curve; `UI/Hud/LowHealthScreenView` drives it.

## Chains

- `Characters/Chains`: `IChainSource` (`ChainOrder`, `ChainCount`, `WriteChain(chain, Span<Vector4>)`) is how a character's tentacle systems feed the VFX chain texture without the texture knowing their assemblies; `Chains.Write` fills a row with positions and a tapered width, `Collapse` hides one, `Pack` lays sources out as rows (unit tested). `Chains.ShowPlaceholderLines` gates the placeholder `LineRenderer`s.

## Feedback

- `Feedback/FeedbackRunner.cs`: `HitStop.Request(seconds)` drops `Time.timeScale` to 2% of its value and restores it on unscaled time (overlapping requests take the longest; `HitStop.Enabled` is the debug toggle); `Rumble.Play(low, high, seconds)` drives `Gamepad.current` motors, gated by `GameSettings.Rumble` and stopped on focus loss; `ScreenShake.Request(origin, strength)` is an event the Camera area turns into impulses. One hidden-in-play `FeedbackRunner` object is created on first use.

## Scenes

- `Settings/SettingsStore` keeps the player's options per profile, not per save slot: `persistentDataPath/settings.json`, loaded before the first scene. `SettingsData` (unit tested; `Clamp` keeps assist ranges: damage taken 0.25–2, dealt 0.5–3, enemy speed 0.5–1) holds the volumes, assists, accessibility toggles, `ColorblindMode`, language code, display (mode, resolution, v-sync, frame cap) and the Input System binding overrides (`InputSystem.actions`).
  - `Apply()` copies it into `GameSettings` (including `EnemySpeedScale` and `Colorblind`), applies bindings and display only when they changed (display not in the editor), sets the locale, and raises `Applied`. `Audio.AudioMix` turns that into bus volumes.
  - `Save()` writes via a temp file and stores the current binding overrides. No toggle-vs-hold option (developer decision).
- `Scenes/SceneFlow` keeps `Bootstrap` loaded and swaps every other scene: `Load(name)` unloads the rest, loads the scene additively and makes it active (so lighting and runtime spawns land in it), `Reload()` reloads the active scene, `Loaded` fires after. `GameBootstrap` (prefab in the `Bootstrap` scene) calls `Load(MainMenu)` on start. `Scenes/LoadGate` (`Hold(owner)` / `Release(owner)`, `IsOpen`) lets loaders (the room streamer) hold back `CreatureSpawner` until the ground has loaded.

## Saves

- `Saves/SaveSystem` (static): `SlotCount` 5, `NewGame(slot)`, `Save()`, `Load(slot)`, `Delete(slot)`, `Summary(slot)` (for slot menus), `Data` (the live `SaveData`) and `Active` (false until a game is started or loaded, so editor play sessions keep prefab defaults). `Save()` asks every registered `ISaveParticipant` to `Capture` into `Data`, adds playtime and the active scene, and writes JSON through `Storage`; `Load` parses (running migrations) and calls `Restore` on every participant. A participant registered while a save is active is restored immediately, so components register in `OnEnable` and unregister in `OnDisable` (`WeaponController` and `AbilityController` save the equipped weapons and ability).
- `SaveData` holds `Progress` (sector, scene, last nest), `Build` (level, the four stats, weapons, ability, mutations), `World` (string-id lists per `WorldFlag`: grown nests, destroyed relays, opened shortcuts, defeated bosses, collected pickups, explored rooms, cleared arenas; `Has`/`Set`/`Clear`) and `Biomass` (carried, plus the dropped amount, scene and position). New systems write their state there and use stable string ids (the scene object's id or name), never instance ids.
- `SaveSerializer.CurrentVersion` is the save format version: bump it when `SaveData` changes shape and register a step with `RegisterMigration(fromVersion, data => ...)`; saves from a newer build are rejected. `ISaveStorage` is the platform seam: `LocalFileStorage` writes `persistentDataPath/Saves/slotN.json` through a temp file and keeps a `.bak`; Steam Cloud plugs in as another implementation.
- `Saves/Autosave` (developer decisions, no manual save): `Autosave.Request(reason)` coalesces requests into one `SaveSystem.SaveAsync()` in `LateUpdate` (capture and JSON on the main thread, about 0.02 ms; the file write on a background task, flushed before the next load, delete, summary and on quit). It only saves while `SaveSystem.Active`, so play checks on an open scene never write files. Wired today: nest grow and rest, biomass reclaim (hooked here), death (`CreatureBiomass`), cache pickups (`BiomassCache`) and arena clears (`ArenaEncounter`); call it from door, shortcut and purchase code as those land. Quitting mid-arena needs nothing extra: non-boss arena state isn't saved and `SaveSystem.ContinueScene` prefers the last nest's scene, where `CreatureSpawner` places the creature.
- Assets referenced from saves are stored by name and resolved through `Data/ContentCatalog.asset` (`ContentCatalog.Find<T>(id)`): add new weapons, abilities and mutations to it, with unique names. F5 Game tab: slot choice, Save, Load, Wipe slot, Open save folder.
- `Progression/Biomass` (static) is the biomass wallet over `SaveSystem.Data.Biomass`: `Add`, `Spend`, `Die(scene, position)` (drops everything carried; the previous drop is lost; nothing happens when `GameSettings.KeepBiomassOnDeath` is on) and `Reclaim()`, with `Changed`, `Dropped` and `Reclaimed` events for HUD and effects. The rules are `Progression/BiomassRules` (pure, unit tested).
- `Progression/Nests` (static): `Grow(id)` marks a nest grown (`WorldFlag.GrownNest`), `Rest(id, scene, position, yaw)` (grown nests only) records it as the respawn point in `Progress` and raises `Rested` (creature heals and refills charge, non-boss arenas refill) and `MenuRequested` (for the nest menu, TV-49); `TryGetRespawn(scene, ...)` is what `CreatureSpawner` uses, and death reloads `RespawnScene`. `StaysCleared(bossArena)` is the arena refill rule.
- `Progression/Stats` (static, over `SaveData.Build`; developer decisions from Progression & Economy and the Balance Sheet): Brutality, Ferocity, Tenacity and Cunning start at 1 (total level 4). `TryLevelUp(kind)` spends `StatRules.LevelCost(total)` (60 + 12 × L^1.4) biomass and adds it to `BiomassSpent` (which sets `SizeStage`), `Respec(b, f, t, c)` is free and keeps the total, `Set` is for debug; `Changed` fires on every change and on load. `StatRules` (pure, unit tested) holds the soft caps (full to 20, half to 40, quarter to 60, bonuses counted above the starting 1) and the per-point values; the live multipliers (`WeaponDamage`, `WeaponStagger`, `AttackSpeed`, `DashRecovery`, `MaxHealthBonus`, `DamageTaken`, `ChargeGain`, `AbilityDamage`, `StatusPotency`) are read where they apply: `Combat.Damage.OutgoingStatModifier` / `IncomingStatModifier` (on the creature's `Health`), `CreatureVitals` (max HP), `CreatureCharge` (`ChargeMeter.GainScale`), `WeaponController` (move time runs at `AttackSpeed`) and `CreatureMotor` (dash cooldown and lockout scale by `DashRecovery`). All bonuses stack multiplicatively. F5 Progression tab: stats, multipliers, level up (spending), free +1/+10, reset. `MutationLoadout` (unit tested): owned (`Build.OwnedMutations`) and equipped (`Build.Mutations`) mutation ids, `Grant`, `Equip(id, cost, used)` (owned, not equipped, and `Fits` the notch pool), `Unequip`, `Changed`. `NotchPool` = 3/5/7/9 by size stage + `Build.BonusNotches` (hidden notches). `ShortcutProgress` (unit tested): `Open(id)` saves `WorldFlag.OpenedShortcut` once and raises `Opened`; `IsFarSide` is the opening-side test. `SecurityRelays` (unit tested): destroyed relays saved as `<system>/<relay>` in `WorldFlag.DestroyedRelay`, `Count(system)`, `RelayDestroyed(system, count)`, `Reset(system)` (debug) and `Power(destroyed, required)` (Armed, PoweringDown at one short, Open). `BossProgress` (unit tested): `Defeat(id, ability)` saves `WorldFlag.DefeatedBoss` and the ability in `Build.Abilities` (raising `AbilityUnlocked` once), `IsDefeated`, `Revive` (debug) and `PhaseFor`, see `Bosses/CLAUDE.md`. `RespecDraft` (unit tested) holds a respec in progress: every stat back at 1, points to assign up to the current total, `Apply` once complete. `MapProgress` (unit tested): explored rooms (`WorldFlag.ExploredRoom`), `Explore`, `Forget`, `Explored`. `Nests.SetRespawn` sets the respawn without raising `Rested` (fast travel). `Collectibles` (unit tested): `Collect(pickupId, kind, itemId, amount)` once per pickup id, `Grant`, `Found` and `WeaponFound` events, `OwnedWeapons`, `Trinkets`. `LoreLogs` (unit tested): `Collect(id)` (in collection order), `Has`, `All`, `Read(id)` raising `ReadRequested`. `Upgrades` (unit tested): Mutagen (`Build.Mutagen`, `AddMutagen`), ability tiers (`Build.AbilityTiers` as `id=tier`; `TryUpgradeAbility(id, cost, maxTier)` spends biomass into `BiomassSpent`) and weapon modifiers (`Build.OwnedModifiers`; `Build.EquippedModifiers` as `weaponId=modifierId`, one per weapon; `TryUnlockModifier(id, biomass, mutagen)`, `GrantModifier`, `EquipModifier(weaponId, id or null)`), with `Changed`.
- `Interaction/Interactions` (static) is the registry of `IInteractable`s (position, radius, `PromptKey` in the `UI` string table, `CanInteract`, `Interact()`): `CreatureConsume` refreshes `Current` (the nearest in range) every frame and calls `TryInteract()` when Grab (B / E) finds nothing to consume, since the controls table has no separate interact button; the HUD's `InteractionPromptView` shows `Current`'s prompt.

## Input

- `Assets/InputSystem_Actions.inputactions` is the project-wide actions asset (Project Settings > Input System). Its `Gameplay` map follows the Controls table on Core Mechanics & Controls: `Move`, `AimStick` (gamepad), `AimPointer` (mouse position), `Weapon1`, `Weapon2`, `Dash`, `Grab`, `Ability`, `Traverse` (LT / F, the contextual traversal button for Grapple and later Breach, Burrow and Vent Squeeze), `Pause` (Start / Esc; not in the rebind list), `Map` (Select / M: tap for the map, hold for the minimap). The `UI` map drives UI Toolkit navigation.
- Read gameplay input through `Input/GameplayInput.Default`, never `Keyboard.current` or `Gamepad.current`, so rebinding keeps working. `Default` is rebuilt on every play start and whenever `InputSystem.actions` changes (domain reload is off, so a static wrapper would otherwise outlive a reimport of the actions asset and throw); never cache it in a field across plays. Buttons expose `Pressed`, `Held` and `Released` for tap, hold and charge logic. `LastDevice` says whether the gamepad or keyboard and mouse was used last, for choosing stick or pointer aim.
- Change actions or bindings through the `InputActionSetupExtensions` API in an eval (load with `InputActionAsset.FromJson`, write `ToJson()` back, reimport), not by hand-editing the JSON. `Tests/GameplayInputTests` checks every action keeps a gamepad and a keyboard and mouse binding.

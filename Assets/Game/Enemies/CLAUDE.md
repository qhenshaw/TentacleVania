# Enemies

Enemy framework (`TentacleVania.Enemies`). Real enemies (Scientist, Security Guard, Handler) are separate tasks built on it; `Debug/TestBrute` (prefab + definition) exercises every attack kind and is only for testing.

## Structure

- `Data/EnemyDefinition` (ScriptableObject): vitals (Balance Sheet HP and armor), move and turn speed, spawn delay, circling range, despawn delay, a list of `EnemyAttack`s (each with its `AnimationHitTime`) and the per-type behaviour values (Scientist panic, flee and cornered distances; Guard preferred range). Enemy prefabs hold no tuning: `Health` and `Consumable` are set from the definition at spawn (their inspectors show the values greyed out through `Combat.IStatsSource`), and every telegraph colour comes from the shared `Data/TelegraphStyle` asset (body glow for dodgeable, undodgeable, hit flash, stunned and feedable, plus the ground indicator colours and lock flash).
- `Data/EnemyAttack`: kind (`Melee` swept arc, `Area` cylinder, `Projectile`), telegraph (`Dodgeable` yellow / `Undodgeable` red), wind-up / active / recovery / cooldown, the target range it's used at, damage, stagger, type, and the shape fields for its kind. `TrackDuringWindUp` is how much of the wind-up the enemy keeps turning to follow the target before committing. `Hits` (default 1) lets one attack hit a target several times, evenly spread over its active window: area attacks re-check their overlap, and melee re-sweeps its arc for each hit.
- `Enemy` is the state machine (`Spawning`, `Chase`, `Circle`, `Attacking`, `Stunned`, `Dead`). Per-enemy behaviour comes from subclasses overriding `TrySelectAttack`, `ChaseDestination`, `CircleDestination`, `OnActiveStart`, `OnDeath` `OnTick` (every AI tick, in any state; use it instead of a per-enemy `Update`) and `AttackAllowed(index)` (boss phases); `IsBoss` hides the overhead bar (e.g. the Scientist flees). `Stun(seconds)` cancels any attack (the stagger task's entry point). Enemies die through `Health.Died` and are destroyed after `DespawnDelay` (pooling comes later); non-boss enemies set `Health.BurstsOnDeath`, so with gore on their renderers hide at death (the gibs replace them), and with gore off they sink over the last 0.8 s (see `Combat/CLAUDE.md`).
- `EnemyManager` is created on first use in the active scene and ticks every enemy from one `Update` (never per-enemy `Update`). It owns the attack tokens and pools projectiles per prefab.
- Movement is a `NavMeshAgent` (rotation driven by `Enemy`). Each room scene bakes its own NavMesh with a `NavMeshSurface` on a `Navigation` object (Default layer only, physics colliders); `CombatArena`'s data is `Scenes/CombatArena/NavMesh-CombatArena.asset` (LFS). Rebake after changing a room's floor or walls.

`AttackKind.Trap` attacks aim `_areaCenter` at the creature (clamped to `MaxRange`), show it as a ground circle like area attacks, and at the active start call `EnemyManager.PlaceTrap` (pooled per trap prefab like projectiles; `ReturnTrap` puts one back); the trap applies the attack's status through the creature's `StatusEffects` when `ContainmentTrap.Contains` (the creature's centre within the radius on the ground plane, unit tested).

Each `EnemyAttack` has optional FMOD events: `WindUpSound` (attack start), `StrikeSound` (active start) and `HitSound` (when it damages something). When copying an enemy prefab whose root has an `Enemy` subclass (`ScientistEnemy`, `GuardEnemy`), add the replacement `Enemy` before removing the old one: `EnemyAnimator` requires an `Enemy`, so the removal fails silently otherwise, and reference the new component (not the old one) from the debug spawn list.

New enemy prefabs are built from the TestBrute layout by an eval (replace the `Enemy` component with the subclass, give it its own CharacterMaster material and definition) and added to `Debug/Resources/DebugEnemySpawnList`.

## Voices

- `EnemyDefinition._voice` points at an `Data/EnemyVoice` asset, one set per type (developer decision): `ScientistVoice`, `GuardVoice` (Security Guard and Lab Security), `HandlerVoice`, `HazmatVoice`, `SurgeonVoice`, `TurretVoice`, `MedDroneVoice`, `WardenVoice` and `AutomatonVoice`. The shared `HumanVoice` and `MachineVoice` remain for the debug enemies. Each set has Alert (first leaving Spawning), Pain (per-enemy `_painCooldown`, 1.2 s), Death (not when consumed), Grabbed (the maw's grab), Attack (every attack wind-up) and Stagger (stagger break; bosses only). Placeholder FMOD events `event:/Enemies/Voice/<Type>/<Moment>` are synth voices pitched per type (machines buzz).
- All go through `EnemyVoices.Play`, which caps voices at 3 per 0.6 s (`VoiceLimiter`, unit tested); death and grabbed are important and always play. F5 Enemies: Enemy voices on/off, Voices played / dropped.

## Placeholder bodies and animation

- Bodies are Kenney Animated Characters (CC0, `Assets/ThirdParty/Kenney/AnimatedCharacters/`, imported as Humanoid so clips retarget between body sizes) under each prefab's `Model` child, scaled to 1.78 m: Scientist medium + businessMaleA, Security Guard medium + militaryMaleA with a pistol on the right hand, Handler large male + cyborg, TestBrute large male + robot (developer decisions). The skin is the `_BaseMap` of the enemy's CharacterMaster material; `CharacterMaterials` lists the skinned mesh (and pistol).
- `EnemyAnimator` (on the root, ticked from `Enemy.TickFeedback`, so it runs even with AI frozen) cross-fades `Animation/EnemyPlaceholder.controller` states by name: `Locomotion` (idle / walk / run blend on agent speed), the attack states named after `EnemyAttack.Animation` (`Swing`, `Punch`, `Kick`, `Shoot`), `Stun` (crouch, also while consumed), `Cower` (`Enemy.IsCowering`, the cornered Scientist, or one `ScientistEnemy.Terrify` pinned in place without attacks, as in the opening) and `Death`. Attack clips play at `EnemyAttack.AnimationHitTime / WindUp` speed so the blow lands exactly when the attack goes active (developer decision); `_hitTimes` holds each clip's hit second, measured as the peak forward reach of the striking hand or foot.
- Hit reactions: a hit from the player (`HitFeedback.ClassifyForPlayer`) pushes the enemy along the hit direction through `NavMeshAgent.Move` (`HitFeedbackLevel.Knockback` × `EnemyDefinition.KnockbackScale`) and `EnemyAnimator.Flinch` leans the `Model` child away from the hit around its feet. Both run from `TickFeedback` on scaled time, so hit-stop freezes them; the stagger-break pose is the `Stun` crouch.
- Kenney rigs carry a 100× scale on their bones: anything parented to a bone (props) needs `localScale = model.lossyScale / bone.lossyScale`, or it renders 45× too big. The pistol model's barrel points along −Z.

## Rules (developer decisions)

- Stagger: each enemy has a `StaggerBar` sized by `EnemyDefinition.MaxStagger` (defaults to its max health); it fills with each hit's `StaggerDamage` and never drains. Breaking it calls `Stun(StaggerStun)` (2.0 s, Balance Sheet) and adds a `StaggerDamageTaken` ×1.5 incoming modifier until the stun ends; the bar can't fill while stunned. The enemy glows pale blue while broken (placeholder for stagger-break poses) and raises `Staggered`. Under the grab threshold (`IsFeedable`) it pulses green through the same status channel (`TelegraphGlow.SetFeedable`, set by `EnemyManager` every frame, even while AI is frozen); the stun glow wins while both apply. Any hit that would leave it under the grab threshold, including the one that crosses it, deals `EnemyDefinition.FeedableDamageTaken` (×0.5, developer decision) health damage, so it lives long enough to be grabbed: the enemy keeps `Health.SetProtection(Consumable.GrabThreshold, scale)` current and the damage pipeline applies it after every modifier and armour, so it stacks with the stagger window's ×1.5. Bosses will set a larger bar and a 1.0 s stun in their definitions.

- Assists and accessibility: the agent speed is `BaseSpeed` (the definition's, or a boss phase's) × `GameSettings.EnemySpeedScale`, set every tick. `TelegraphStyle` swaps its dodgeable, undodgeable, feedable and indicator colours for the palette matching `GameSettings.Colorblind` (Protanopia and Deuteranopia: yellow vs violet-blue with a cyan feed glow; Tritanopia: pink vs red).
- Attack tokens: at most 2 melee/area and 2 ranged attacks winding up or active at once (`EnemyManager.MeleeTokens` / `RangedTokens`). Enemies that want to attack without a token circle at `CircleRange`.
- Telegraphs: the whole enemy glows yellow or red, ramping from 25% to full across the wind-up (`TelegraphGlow`, through the enemy's `CharacterMaterials` on the CharacterMaster shader). Ground telegraphs (developer decisions): `EnemyAttack.GroundIndicator` (`Auto` shows one for area, projectile and red attacks; `On`/`Off` override; F5 Enemies "Force ground indicators" shows every attack's) drives the enemy's `GroundIndicator`, one URP `DecalProjector` with an instance of `Telegraphs/Materials/TelegraphDecal.mat` (procedural `TelegraphDecal` Shader Graph, a Decal-target spec: `_Shape` circle / lane / sector, `_Arc`, `_Size` in metres, `_Progress`, `_Lock`). Area attacks draw a circle at the hit radius (the hit is an overlap cylinder at the same center and radius), melee a sector of `Reach + SweepRadius` and `Arc` from the enemy's feet, projectiles a `LaneWidth` lane from the muzzle ending at the nearest of `MaxRange`, a wall (`EnemyLayers.WallMask`) or 1 m past the creature. The outline shows the full shape from the start and the fill sweeps outward, reaching the edge as the attack goes active; the shape follows the aim while tracking, then freezes when tracking ends (`AttackMath.IsLocked`) and its outline flashes and stays brighter. Shots fire along the locked heading (`AttackMath.LockedAim`, pitched to the creature's aim height), so leaving the lane after the lock dodges them. Red attacks set `HitFlags.Undodgeable`, which the creature's dash i-frames don't block.
- Collision: enemies are solid to the walking creature (capsule on the `Enemy` layer); the creature's dash excludes the `Enemy` layer, so it passes through.
- Melee hits are swept: sphere casts from the enemy outward along the arc, subdivided to at most 10° per cast so fast swings never skip a target. Projectiles are pooled kinematic-free rigidbodies with trigger colliders aimed at `CombatTargets.CreatureAimPoint`; a dash-blocked projectile flies on.

## Pitfalls

- Enemy materials must use the CharacterMaster shader and the prefab needs a `CharacterMaterials` listing its renderers, or telegraphs, hit flashes and the stun glow won't show. (URP Lit can't be used: it drops `_EMISSION` at runtime when the base emission is black.)
- Ground decals project through everything in their box, so the indicator box is a 0.6 m slice at floor level; use decal layers if it needs to skip characters entirely.

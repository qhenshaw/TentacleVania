# Creature

The player creature (`TentacleVania.Creature`). The prefab `Prefabs/Creature.prefab` is spawned at runtime by a `CreatureSpawner` in the scene (`CombatArena` has one at the origin); `CreatureSpawner.Spawned` returns the instance for evals.

## Movement (`Movement/`)

- `CreatureMotor` drives a `CharacterController` from `GameplayInput`; all tuning lives in a `CreatureMotorSettings` asset (`Data/HatchlingMotor.asset`, Balance Sheet baselines: 7 m/s, 4 m dash, 0.3 s cooldown chained twice then 0.8 s lockout, 0.2 s i-frames).
- Snappy acceleration (120 m/s²). Movement is camera-relative. The maw faces the aim (mouse pointer on the creature's plane, or the right stick past the deadzone, by `GameplayInput.LastDevice`), otherwise the move direction.
- A dash covers exactly `DashDistance` over `DashDuration` (0.15 s, not in the design), in the move direction or the facing when there is no move input, then drops straight to walking speed. Presses up to 0.1 s early are buffered. The cooldown counts from the end of the dash; `DashTimer` holds the chain and lockout rules and is unit tested.
- Hooks for later systems: `TryDash(direction)` for attack dash-cancels, `IsInvulnerable` for the damage pipeline, `InputEnabled` to lock control (consume, stuns), and the `Dashed` event.
- The body is a placeholder knot (a small dark sphere, 0.3 m across, plus a red maw marking the front); the `CharacterController` hurtbox (0.45 m radius) is larger than the body and covers the leg span.

## Procedural legs (`Rig/`)

- `CreatureLegs` (runs in `LateUpdate` after the motor) builds one chain per leg from a `CreatureRigSettings` asset per size stage (`Data/HatchlingRig.asset`: 6 thin legs of 5 bones, 1.1 m long, feet resting 0.66 m out, about 1.3 m across). `Build(settings)` rebuilds it for another stage; stages must stay under `CreatureRigSettings.MaxChains` (20) counting free and weapon tentacles.
- Each frame a chain is seeded spider-style (`ChainSolver.SeedArch`: two straight segments meeting at a sharp knee 30% of the way out and `ArchHeight` (0.5 m) above the root, at the joint 40% along the chain, developer decision) and solved with FABRIK (`ChainSolver.Solve`), so legs bow up and out instead of flipping. Feet are ground-probed with `RaycastNonAlloc`, skipping the creature's own collider.
- Gait (`LegGait.SelectSteps`, unit tested): a leg steps when its foot drifts past `StepThreshold` from its rest point, largest drift first, never while a neighbor is stepping, and with at most half the legs lifted. A foot past `EmergencyDistance` steps regardless. Steps land ahead by `PredictTime` × velocity, clamped to leg reach, so feet keep up at 7 m/s; during a dash the legs trail and catch up. `StepDuration` is the slow-walk step: faster movement shortens steps to fit the stride the leg reach allows, or every leg emergency-steps at once.
- Organic layer (settings under "Organic motion"; debug toggle "Organic gait" turns it off for comparison): a fixed-seed per-leg spread of angle, rest radius, pace, lift and threshold, plus random scatter and timing per step; an asymmetric lift (high early, slapping down) that swings out from the body; a Perlin wave travelling down each leg after the solve; and the `_bodyParts` (Body, Maw) bobbing on each plant, leaning away from lifted legs and breathing, with leg roots following them. The body offset is an underdamped spring (`BodySpringFrequency`, `BodySpringDamping`, substepped at 120 Hz) pushed against the creature's acceleration by `BodyInertia`, so it lurches forward and bounces back on stopping and trails on turns; `BodyMaxOffset` caps it (dashes hit the cap).
- Legs render as tapered `LineRenderer`s as a placeholder until the VFX Graph strips and tube shader replace them (hide them with the Creature debug toggle "Placeholder tentacle lines").
- VFX chain feed (developer decisions): `Rig/ChainTexture` (on the prefab, runs last in `LateUpdate`) packs every `Core.Characters.IChainSource` on the creature into a `Texture2D` (RGBAFloat, point-filtered) of `Chains.Joints` (6) × chain count: one row per chain in `ChainOrder` (legs 0, weapon tentacles 10, consume tentacles 20), each pixel a world-space joint with the strip width in alpha (tapering root to tip). Hidden chains (weapon tentacle not attacking, consume tentacles idle) collapse onto their root with width 0. It sets `ChainPositions`, `ChainCount` and `ChainLength` on the root `VisualEffect` (`Assets/Game/VFX/CreatureTentacles/CreatureTentacles.vfx`, the developer's), rebuilding the texture when the chain count changes. Every chain has exactly `Chains.Joints` joints, so `CreatureRigSettings` no longer sets bones per leg. The gameplay hurtbox stays the `CharacterController`, never the legs.
- `SpringChain` (unit tested) is the Verlet chain for every non-leg tentacle: each step pulls joints toward a pose (stiff at the root, looser toward the tip by `TipLooseness`), pins the root (and the tip when asked) and restores bone lengths with FABRIK passes. A bone folded more than ~100° from its pose direction snaps back to it, since symmetric constraints can lock a collinear zigzag.
- Free tentacles (`CreatureTentacles`, chain order 5, settings under "Free tentacles" in the rig asset; Hatchling has 3): a fan behind and above the body, posed with a Perlin sway and simulated on `SpringChain`, so they trail and whip with movement. Their roots follow the legs' body spring (`CreatureLegs.BodyOffset`). Debug toggle "Free tentacles".
- The solver is plain managed code over preallocated arrays (no per-frame allocations). Move it to a Burst job if chain counts at Apex make it show up in the profiler.

## Control locks and stun

`CreatureMotor.InputEnabled` is true while no system holds a lock: call `LockControl()` / `UnlockControl()` in pairs (death, the consume root and stuns each hold one), never set it directly. A hit whose `DamageInfo.StunDuration` > 0 stuns the creature through `CreatureVitals.Stun` (control locked, electric-blue status pulse, the charge glow restored afterwards). Stuns don't stack or extend: while stunned and for 3 s after it ends (`_stunGrace`, developer decision) further stuns are ignored, though their damage still lands. Debug: Creature tab Stun state and "Stun 1 s".

## Grab & consume

`CreatureConsume` (developer decisions): Grab (B / E) takes the nearest enemy with a `Combat.Consumable` under 25% HP within 2.2 m, preferring ones in front; with nothing grabbable the press does nothing. The creature roots for 0.4 s while three spring-chain tentacles shoot out (first 30%), coil around the victim and drag it into the maw (it shrinks as a placeholder dissolve, then `FinishConsume` kills it), then is free again while the heal flows: `Consumable.HealFraction` of the creature's max HP over 1.5 s, with a ×0.5 damage-taken modifier for the whole 1.5 s. Each consume adds `Consumable.Biomass` to `Biomass` and +20 charge. The creature publishes its threshold as `Consumable.GrabThreshold` (read by `Consumable.IsGrabbable` for the HUD and enemy glow). Grabbed enemies enter `EnemyState.Consumed` (no AI, colliders and agent off) so nothing else can hit them. Heal and biomass per enemy live in `EnemyDefinition` (Balance Sheet values).

## Charge meter

`CreatureCharge` (on the prefab) wraps a `Combat.ChargeMeter`: max 100, +3 for every hit the creature lands (`CombatEvents.HitLanded` with the creature as attacker, hazards excluded), +20 from `AddConsume()` (for grab & consume). Abilities call `TrySpend(cost)` (35–60). `ChargeMeter.GainScale` is the hook for Cunning's +2% charge gain. When full, the creature pulses teal through `CharacterMaterials.SetStatus` (Art Direction's charge-full bioluminescence), body and tentacles alike.

## Testing in play

`unity-play-capture.sh` with `TestGamepad.Set(move, aim, buttons...)` drives it; read `CreatureSpawner.Spawned` and its `CreatureMotor` (`Velocity`, `IsDashing`, `DashChainCount`) from `--at` files. Keep values across `--at` files with `UnityEditor.SessionState`. Virtual gamepad input only survives while Player Settings' Run In Background is on.

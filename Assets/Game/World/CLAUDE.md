# World

Rooms and encounters (`TentacleVania.World`).

## CombatArena (Combat Prototype)

- `Prefabs/CombatArena_Room.prefab` is the greybox room (developer decisions): a 20×20 m floor centred on the origin, full-height back wall, 3 m side walls, low (1 m) front wall facing the camera, four 2×2×3 m pillars at (±5, ±4), a 4×8 m entry corridor to the south (the creature spawns at z = −15) and a 4×4 m exit stub to the north. Greybox materials (`EnvironmentMaster`, so they dither-fade when they hide the creature) are in `Materials/`; characters never use them. A URP Lit material given `_EMISSION` from an eval loses the keyword the next time the editor validates it, so glowing props (warning lights) use the URP Particles/Unlit shader with an HDR `_BaseColor` instead.
- Two `ArenaDoor`s: south (starts open, 1 m slab) and north (sealed, 4 m slab). Doors slide into the floor with PrimeTween; the slab's collider is the blocker. The NavMesh was baked with both doors closed, so enemies never path into the corridor; rebake after moving geometry (see the Enemies CLAUDE.md).
- `ArenaEncounter` on the room root: when the creature enters the trigger box (18×17 m inside the walls) both doors seal and the waves in `Data/CombatArenaWaves.asset` run. Each wave's enemies are shuffled and spawned 0.3 s apart round-robin at six wall hatches (`SpawnPoints`), then walk in; the next wave starts 2 s after the last enemy dies (consumed or killed). After the last wave both doors open; nothing else happens (developer decision).
- Arenas can have wall mounts (`_wallMounts`; `CombatArena_Room` has `WallMounts/WestMount` and `EastMount` on the side walls): enemies whose `EnemyDefinition.SpawnOnWall` is set (turrets) spawn there in turn instead of at the hatches.
- Waves (developer-approved): 1: 3 scientists + 1 guard; 2: 2 guards + 1 handler + 2 scientists; 3: 2 handlers + 2 guards + 1 scientist; 4: 2 handlers + 3 guards + 2 scientists.
- Scene: `CombatArena` holds the room instance, the `GameplayCamera`, `CameraBounds` (21×29 m around room and corridor), `CreatureSpawn`, `Navigation` and the light.
- Debug: the World tab shows the arena state and has Start arena now and Skip wave (kill all). `Tools/UnityCheck/evals/gc-profile-start.cs` starts the arena with god mode on, so `unity-gc-profile.sh` profiles real combat.
- `BioNests/BioNest` (prefab `BioNests/BioNest.prefab`, developer decisions from Progression & Economy and Core Mechanics): a marked nest site (placeholder dark disc) that Grab grows for free into a nest (placeholder pink pods, `NestGrow` burst and FMOD event). It's an `IHoldInteractable` (developer decisions): a tap grows a site and/or sets the nest as the checkpoint (`SetCheckpoint`: `Nests.SetRespawn`, autosave, rest burst; no heal, no menu, no arena refill); holding Grab for 0.6 s (`RestHoldDuration`) grows it if needed and rests. Resting goes through `Core.Progression.Nests.Rest` with the `RespawnPoint` child (1.8 m in front). Save id: `_id`, or `<scene>/<object name>`. `CombatArena` has `BioNest_Entry` at the south end of the corridor. Resting records the active scene (the sector root when rooms stream) as the respawn scene. `ArenaEncounter` resets to Waiting (enemies removed, doors back to their start state) on `Nests.Rested` unless it's a boss arena (`_bossArena`), which saves `WorldFlag.ClearedArena` on clear and starts open and empty from then on. World tab: last nest, Grow all nests, Rest at nearest nest, Teleport to nearest nest.
- `Shortcuts/Shortcut` (prefabs `ShortcutTunnel`, a low crawl grate that lifts, and `OneWayDoor`, a slab that sinks; design: Core Mechanics, Respawns and Progression & Economy, Traversal rules):
  - **Opening:** it opens only from the far side, where its `Lever` child stands; the lever's forward points away from the blocker. It's an `IInteractable` (`prompt.shortcut.open`) whose `CanInteract` checks `ShortcutProgress.IsFarSide` against the creature. It can also open when the nest in `_openOnNestGrown` grows.
  - **Saving:** it saves through `Core.Progression.ShortcutProgress` (`WorldFlag.OpenedShortcut`, with an autosave), plays `event:/World/Shortcut/Unlock`, and starts open when saved.
  - **Placement:** never place one inside an arena.
  - **Debug:** `Shortcuts/Debug/Resources/DebugShortcutRig.prefab` (a wall with a tunnel and a door, far side +Z). World tab: shortcuts open, Open all, Close all, Spawn shortcut test rig.
- `Security/` (design: Progression & Economy, Security system): the boss arena is sealed by a `SecurityDoor` until all three `SecurityRelay`s of its system are destroyed.
  - **Relay** (`SecurityRelay.prefab`): a destructible cabinet on the `Enemy` layer, so weapons and soft-lock hit it. It has its own `Health` (60 HP placeholder, `Bleeds` off) and a `Hurtbox`. Death saves it through `Core.Progression.SecurityRelays.Destroy(system, id)` and swaps to the `Broken` child, with the `RelaySparks` burst and the `event:/World/Security/RelayDestroyed` sound. Saved relays start broken.
  - **Door** (`SecurityDoor.prefab`): Armed until 2 of 3 relays are gone; then PoweringDown, with its warning lights flickering and the `PowerDown` sound; then it slides its slab into the floor and drops its blocker at 3/3 (`DoorOpen`). It snaps to its saved state on `Start`.
  - **Cables:** each door `Cable` (relay id, a `LineRenderer` on `TurretLaser.mat`, a lamp) goes dark when its relay dies. They are authored per placement, from the door toward each relay room.
  - **Hooks:** `SecurityRelays.RelayDestroyed(system, count)` is the hook for the PA lines and the map counter (`SecurityRelays.Count(system)`).
  - **Debug:** `Security/Debug/Resources/DebugSecurityRig.prefab` (door plus relays A, B, C of system `Debug`). World tab: security readout, Spawn security test rig, Destroy nearest relay, Reset relays.
- Enemies are instantiated per wave; pool them when a room's spawn counts make it show up in the profiler.

## Pickups and lore

- `Pickups/Pickup` collects when the creature walks within `_radius` (no physics): `Core.Progression.Collectibles.Collect(saveId, kind, itemId, amount)` saves `WorldFlag.CollectedPickup` once (id `_id` or `<scene>/<object name>`, read in `OnEnable`, which hides a collected one), grants the item and autosaves. Kinds: Biomass (cache, 150 placeholder until TV-32's economy table), Mutagen, Trinket (`Build.Trinkets`, for the Hoarder), Weapon (`Build.OwnedWeapons`; `WeaponController` equips it into an empty slot), Mutation (`MutationLoadout.Grant`), Notch (`Build.BonusNotches`). Prefabs in `Pickups/` (spinning unlit primitives, `event:/World/Pickup/Collect`): `BiomassCache`, `MutagenPickup`, `TrinketPickup`, `CrusherMassPickup`, `MutationPickup` (Wide Dodge), `NotchPickup`. Mutagen beyond the 16 modifiers isn't converted to biomass yet.
- `Lore/LoreLog` assets (`Lore/Data/`, id = asset name; text in the `Dialogue` table as `lore.<id>.title`, `.from`, `.body`; Text or Audio with an FMOD `_audio` event, played by `LoreAudio`) are listed in order in `Lore/Resources/LoreLibrary`. `Lore/LoreTerminal.prefab` (an `IInteractable`, Grab: `prompt.lore.read`) collects its log (`Core.Progression.LoreLogs`, `WorldFlag.CollectedLog`) and raises `LoreLogs.Read`, which opens the UI reader; its green screen shows until the log is read. The 8 slice logs are all Text and not yet placed (the room plans don't say which room holds which).
- `Hoarder/HoarderMerchant` (`Hoarder/Hoarder.prefab`: a greybox vent with two bobbing capsule hands; an `IInteractable`, Grab: `prompt.hoarder.trade`, chitters `event:/World/Hoarder/Chitter`) raises `TradeRequested` for the UI trade screen. Its `HoarderStock` (`SliceHoarderStock`: Gluttony unlocked by the Pod Release Key trinket, Rapid Digestion by the Tagged Locker Key; trinket ids `PodReleaseKey`, `TaggedLockerKey`) lists mutation, unlocking trinket (empty = always in stock) and price (0 = `Core.Progression.Shop.MutationPrice` by notches: 300 / 700 / 1,500). `Shop.TryBuyMutation` spends biomass into `BiomassSpent`, grants the mutation and autosaves; an owned mutation leaves the stock.
- F5 World: Spawn Hoarder, Give slice trinkets, Lore logs, Weapons / trinkets, Spawn pickup row, Spawn lore terminal (per log), Collect all lore logs (`Pickups/Resources/DebugPickupSet`). A pickup spawned from an eval gets its save id in `OnEnable`, inside `Instantiate`: rename it, then disable and enable it.

## Sectors (generated)

- `Editor/SectorBuilder` builds a sector from a `SectorPlan` (`Editor/SectorPlan.cs`: rooms with grid rects, hallway paths, openings, role and waves, plus a `Place` callback for content). Per room it makes a scene `<Folder>/<Scene>.unity`: a root with `Room` (`_sector`), a `Layout` child (`LevelLayout` on `SciFiTopDown`, `NavMeshSurface` on its children's physics colliders) holding the `LayoutRoom`, `LayoutHallway`s (1 cell wide) and `LayoutOpening`s, `CameraBounds` over the scene's cells + 6 m, and for arena roles an `ArenaEncounter` (waves asset `Data/Sectors/<Sector>/<Scene>_Waves.asset`, 8 inset spawn points, optional wall mounts, trigger over the room inset 1.5 m, `_bossArena` for `PlanRole.Boss`, id `<Sector>/<Scene>`). Arena openings use `Arenas/ArenaDoorCell.prefab` (3 m slab, starts open); `ArenaEncounter` collects the doors of its own scene when `_doors` is empty, and only a fighting arena claims `ArenaEncounter.Active`. Then it bakes the NavMesh synchronously (`NavMeshSurface.BuildNavMesh`) and saves it as `<Folder>/NavMesh-<Scene>.asset` (LFS by name; `LayoutGenerator.BakeNavMesh` bakes asynchronously, so a scene saved and closed right after it ships with no NavMesh and enemies can't move in builds), fills the waves asset in place and saves it (an asset edited through `SerializedObject` right after `CreateAsset` can reach disk empty), updates the sector registry (`<Folder>/<Root>Rooms.asset`, Addressables group = sector name, included in builds), copies the `StreamingTest` root as the sector root on first build (`CreatureSpawn` at the plan's spawn) and adds the registry to `MapAtlas`.
- **Cross-scene joins:** each hallway belongs to one room scene; where a hallway or room meets a space in another scene, both scenes put a `Doorway` (or arena door) opening on the shared edge. `LayoutGrid` treats an opening on an outer edge (no space beyond it in this layout) as open, so each scene leaves its side of the join free of walls and corner pieces. Rebuilding overwrites the scenes (hand edits are lost); add content in the plan's `Place` callbacks. Build one room with `SpecimenHoldingPlan.Build("SH_A1")` (close that scene first: a scene can't be overwritten while open).
- **Specimen Holding** (`Editor/SpecimenHoldingPlan`, menu TentacleVania > World > Build Specimen Holding; developer decisions): the room plan on a 3 m grid (standard arenas 7×7 cells, ambush 5×5, Warden arena 8×6), pod chamber (2 scientists in a one-time encounter: `ArenaEncounter._oneTime` saves it cleared like a boss arena so resting doesn't bring them back; `SH/PodNest`, the `PodExitDoor` (`BioNests/NestDoor.prefab`: a plain slab with a red lamp that sinks and turns green once its `_nestId` is grown, starting open when it already is; developer decision: the pod exit stays locked until the first nest grows), PodHistory and PodLabel terminals, the `sh.entry` PA zone), A1–A8 with the room plan's waves (turret rooms get wall mounts), relays A3/A5/A7 (system `SH`), Crusher Mass in A8, NightShiftChat in the A1–A2 corridor, R1 (Pod Release Key) off A2, R2 (biomass cache, IntakeMemo) off A6, the Grapple room (Mutagen) off A4 behind a 3 m chasm with anchors and a `MapGate`, the pre-boss nest room (`SH/PreBossNest`) with the security door in its corridor, the Warden arena (boss wave, `WardenHall` 24×18, freight elevator powered by `SH/WardenDrone`, no destination until Bio-Labs exists), shortcuts T2 (A1 ↔ A6, opens from A6) and T1 (pod chamber ↔ pre-boss nest, opens when `SH/PreBossNest` grows) as crawlspace pairs - **Bio-Labs** (`Editor/BioLabsPlan`, menu TentacleVania > World > Build Bio-Labs; room plan on the same grid): entry nest room (`BL/EntryNest`, the Hoarder, the freight elevator back to the Warden arena, the `bl.entry` PA zone), A1–A9 with the room plan's waves, relays A3/A7/A9 (system `BL`), A5 dissection theatre (SiblingTermination, SurgeonsBettingPool), TissueSampleSource in the A4–A5 corridor, R1 (Tagged Locker Key) off A2, R2 (biomass cache, DissectionSchedule) off A4, R3 (Lunging Maw) off A8, the Vent Squeeze room (Mutagen) off A6 behind a sealed grate and `MapGate`, the pre-boss nest (`BL/PreBossNest`) with the security door, and the Automaton theatre (boss wave, `AutomatonTheatre` radius 11 with a carving table). Its shortcuts T1 (A5 ↔ entry), T2 (A9 ↔ A3) and T3 (pre-boss ↔ A4) are `Shortcuts/Crawlspace` pairs, since tunnels across the 3×3 grid would cross corridors in other scenes (crossing hallways of different scenes wall each other off).
- `Shortcuts/Crawlspace` (`Crawlspace.prefab`: a floor-level grate against a wall; an `IInteractable`): two ends share a `ShortcutProgress` id; the end with `_opensHere` (the far side) opens it (`prompt.shortcut.open`), then either end crawls through (`prompt.crawlspace.enter`) as a tunnel transit (developer decisions): `Map.TunnelRoute` (unit tested) routes from the entrance through the centres of the rooms between the ends (breadth-first over the registry's `Neighbours`), and `FastTravel.Tunnel` moves the creature along it 3 m under the floor at 40 m/s (`TunnelSpeed`, no cap) with the motor suspended (`Core.Characters.ICreatureTransit`), so floors hide it, the camera and streaming follow it and arenas on the way don't trigger. A glowing `TrailRenderer` (`Map/Resources/TunnelTrail.mat`) follows on the floor and the transition shows a dark vignette; once the streamer is idle the creature appears at the exit (the other end + 1.2 m into its room). `_openOnNestGrown` opens a crawlspace when that nest grows. Outside a streamed sector it falls back to `FastTravel.Crawl` (a covered teleport).
- `RoomRegistryTools.Update` also bakes each room's `MapShapes` (layout room rects and hallway cells) so the map draws real floor outlines.

## Map and fast travel

- Each `RoomRegistry` also holds the sector's map data: `_sector` (id, named by the `Dialogue` table's `sector.<id>`), `_rootScene` (what fast travel loads), `_color`, and `_markers`, baked by `RoomRegistryTools.Update` from the room scenes: `BioNest` (id `SaveId`, respawn point and yaw), `SecurityRelay` (`<system>/<id>`), `SecurityDoor`, `Map/MapGate` (place one at every ability gate; the map shows a generic blocked mark) and `HoarderMerchant`. Rerun the update after placing any of them.
- `Map/Resources/MapAtlas` lists every sector registry (only `StreamingTestRooms` until the slice sectors exist; add theirs). `RoomStreamer` marks the current room explored (`Core.Progression.MapProgress`, `WorldFlag.ExploredRoom`).
- `Map/FastTravel.Travel(nestId)` (grown nests only): `Nests.SetRespawn` to the marker, then `FastTravelRunner` (persistent) runs Covering (0.6 s unscaled) → `SceneFlow.Load(rootScene)` → Loading (until the load gate opens) → Revealing (0.8 s) → Idle, exposing `Cover` (0–1) for the UI transition, resetting the time scale to 1 and autosaving on arrival. The creature spawns at the nest through the normal respawn.
- F5 World: Map (explored rooms, markers, travel phase), Reveal sector map, Forget sector map, Grow every atlas nest. The `StreamingTest` rooms have test nests in Room 1 and Room 5 and a `MapGate` in Room 3.

## Room streaming

- A streamed sector is a root scene (light, `CreatureSpawn`, `GameplayCamera`, `CombatHud`, volume, `RoomStreamer`) plus one scene per arena or corridor, loaded additively through Addressables (address = scene name). Room scenes are not in Build Settings.
- **Registry:** `Streaming/RoomRegistry` (one per sector) lists each room's scene, XZ bounds and neighbours, plus `LoadRadius` (18 m) and `UnloadRadius` (28 m; the gap keeps edge rooms from reloading). Fill it with `RoomRegistryTools.Update(registry, group, includeInBuild)` (menu TentacleVania > World > Update Selected Room Registry, group `Rooms`): it measures each scene's renderers, links rooms whose bounds touch (within 1 m), adds the scenes to the Addressables group and removes them from Build Settings. Rerun it after moving a room. Pass the sector's own Addressables group (`StreamingTest` for the test chain): a new group name moves the scenes into a new group.
- **Rules:** `Streaming/RoomStreamingRules` (pure, unit tested). The current room is the one containing the creature; it sticks while the creature stands on a shared edge. The current room and its neighbours always load; others load within `LoadRadius` and unload past `UnloadRadius`.
- **Streamer:** `RoomStreamer` (in the root scene) ticks every 0.2 s from `CameraFocus.Target`, or before the spawn from the nest respawn or its `_spawnPoint`. It holds `Core.Scenes.LoadGate` until the first rooms finish loading; `CreatureSpawner` waits for the gate, so the creature never spawns over nothing. `RoomStreamer.CurrentRoomChanged` and `CurrentScene` report the room.
- **Per room:** each room scene's root has a `Room` that enables its `CameraBounds` only while it's current, so camera bounds hand over at room boundaries. Its `_sector` id (`SH`, `BL`: the `Dialogue` table's `sector.<id>` keys) becomes `Progress.Sector` when the room is current (or on enable with no streamer), which the save slot list shows; no room sets one yet. Each room bakes its own NavMesh (`NavMeshSurface` on its `Geometry`, Children, PhysicsColliders, data next to the scene). NavMeshes of neighbouring rooms don't link, so enemies stay in their room.
- **Nests and reloads:** `BioNest` records the active scene, the sector root, as the respawn scene, so death and Continue reload the whole sector.
- **Leaving:** a `SceneFlow` scene change unloads the rooms with everything else; Addressables then warns once, "Failed to remove scene from Addressables profiler", which is harmless.
- **Test chain:** `Scenes/StreamingTest/StreamingTest.unity` with `StreamingTest_Room1`–`5` (arena, corridor, arena, corridor, arena along +Z) and `StreamingTestRooms.asset`, in the Addressables group `StreamingTest` (not included in builds). In the editor every room loads within a frame or two, so check hitches in a build.
- **Debug:** World tab "Rooms" (current room, in-flight loads, each room's state), Pause room streaming, Load all rooms, Unload distant rooms.

## Level layout tool (editor only)

Non-destructive room builder (developer decisions; the wiki's Room Dressing Tool page is the spec). Runtime data in `Layout/` (`TentacleVania.World.Layout`), editor code in `Editor/` (`TentacleVania.World.Editor`, the first area editor assembly), unit tests in `Tests/`.

- `LevelLayout` (one per room scene) holds a `Tileset`, a `DressingSet` and child shapes: `LayoutRoom` (grid `RectInt`s; overlapping or touching rects of one room merge), `LayoutHallway` (cell path with 90° bends and a width in cells) and `LayoutOpening` (cell + side + width, kind `Doorway`, `Door` from the tileset, or any `Prefab` such as an arena door). Grid size is the tileset's `CellSize` (3 m for `SciFiTopDown`). The shapes are the source of truth: `Generated` is deleted and rebuilt on every change; never edit it. Hand-placed art goes under `Manual`, which the tool never touches and keeps props away from.
- `LayoutGrid` (pure, tested) owns the rules: room cells win over hallway cells; edges between different rooms are walls unless an opening joins them; a hallway edge is open where the hallway runs into the neighbouring space (so a hallway end opens into a room, but one running alongside it doesn't); hallways joining each other open the same way.
- `Tileset`: a floor slot and any number of `WallLayer`s (here Walls, Wall Tops, Columns), each with Straight, Corner (replaces both walls of a convex corner cell), ConvexPost and ConcavePost slots, plus a Door slot. Entries carry weight, offset, rotation and scale in a canonical pose (see the tileset inspector note: walls on the cell's north edge, corners north+east, posts at the north-east vertex). Pack pivots differ (this pack's floors pivot at a corner, walls at another), so calibrate entries with the tileset inspector's "Preview in scene" (a `DontSave` sample layout at 200,0,200 that rebuilds on every edit) and `unity-capture.sh`. `Spawn` keeps the prefab root's own rotation (the pack's wall tops carry 270°).
- Variants are seeded per space (`LayoutRandom`, stable across rebuilds; Reroll changes the seed); `FloorPins`/`WallPins` on a space force a variant.
- `DressingSet`: props with zones (Wall, Corner, Center, NearDoor), category (WalkThrough: colliders off; Dynamic: on the `Props` layer, which ignores Creature and Enemy; Cover: blocking, capped by `MaxCoverPerSpace`), footprint radius, weight; per-zone density and spacing; openings keep `DoorClearance` clear; room cells walled on two opposite sides get no props. Hallways (developer decision) only get Wall-zone props no larger than `HallwayMaxRadius` (0.35 m), against their walls, never corner, centre or near-door props (the sample set's small props are the pipe and the short vent). Lock selected generated props from the overlay to pin them (`PinnedProps` on their space, survive rerolls); Unlock removes the pin.
- Editing: select the layout or any shape for the Scene View "Level Layout" overlay (add Room / Hallway / Opening, Rebuild, Rebuild + bake, Reroll all, Lock/Unlock props, Manual group). Selected shapes get grid-snapped handles (room edge sliders and centre move, hallway points, opening edge); releasing a handle rebuilds when `AutoRegenerate` is on. Each room, hallway and opening draws a pickable gizmo (room rects, hallway cells, an opening marker), so clicking a shape's area in the Scene View selects it; the generator disables picking on the generated floors, walls and doors (`SceneVisibilityManager`) so those clicks reach the gizmos, while props stay pickable for Lock props. The layout gizmo draws walls (white), openings (yellow) and open joins (green). Generated tiles are marked static; Rebuild + bake rebakes the scene's `NavMeshSurface`s (`LayoutSandbox` has one, PhysicsColliders on Default).
- Assets: `Data/Layout/SciFiTopDown_Tileset.asset` and `SciFiTopDown_Dressing.asset` (third-party pack `Assets/ThirdParty/SciFiTopDown/`). Only straight walls with the 0.87 m footprint are in the tileset (Wall_02 and 19–22 are thicker); the bonus floors pivot on the opposite corner (offset +1.5, −1.5); the pack's doors are single leaves, so `Prefabs/Layout/Door_1_Pair` and `Door_2_Pair` put a left and right leaf either side of the cell centre (Door_3 is 3.9 m wide, too wide for one cell). `Panel_01` is a curved trim piece, not a floor plate. Not yet: 45° diagonals and camera-facing low walls (wiki spec).

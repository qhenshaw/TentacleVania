# World

Rooms and encounters (`TentacleVania.World`).

## CombatArena (Combat Prototype)

- `Prefabs/CombatArena_Room.prefab` is the greybox room (developer decisions): a 20×20 m floor centred on the origin, full-height back wall, 3 m side walls, low (1 m) front wall facing the camera, four 2×2×3 m pillars at (±5, ±4), a 4×8 m entry corridor to the south (the creature spawns at z = −15) and a 4×4 m exit stub to the north. Greybox materials (`EnvironmentMaster`, so they dither-fade when they hide the creature) are in `Materials/`; characters never use them. A URP Lit material given `_EMISSION` from an eval loses the keyword the next time the editor validates it, so glowing props (warning lights) use the URP Particles/Unlit shader with an HDR `_BaseColor` instead.
- Two `ArenaDoor`s: south (starts open, 1 m slab) and north (sealed, 4 m slab). Doors slide into the floor with PrimeTween; the slab's collider is the blocker. The NavMesh was baked with both doors closed, so enemies never path into the corridor; rebake after moving geometry (see the Enemies CLAUDE.md).
- `ArenaEncounter` on the room root: when the creature enters the trigger box (18×17 m inside the walls) both doors seal and the waves in `Data/CombatArenaWaves.asset` run. Each wave's enemies are shuffled and spawned 0.3 s apart round-robin at six wall hatches (`SpawnPoints`), then walk in; the next wave starts 2 s after the last enemy dies (consumed or killed). After the last wave both doors open; nothing else happens (developer decision). `ArenaSession` remembers cleared arena ids for the session, so a room that streams out and back in (walking away, tunnels, elevators) starts cleared with its doors open. It forgets them on `Nests.Rested` and on every `SceneFlow.Loading` (death restart, nest travel, menu), so only nests (and deaths) refill rooms (developer decision).
- Lockdown feedback (developer decisions): `Arenas/ArenaLockdown` (one per sector root, added by `SectorBuilder.AddLockdown`) plays `event:/World/Arena/Klaxon` twice and dims the sun to 40% while any arena is fighting, then plays `AllClear` and restores it. `Arenas/ArenaBeacon.prefab` (one above each arena door) spins a red light while its arena fights and turns steady amber (`BeaconAmber.mat`) when it clears. `ArenaEncounter.AnyCleared` carries `HadHumans` so the toast says "Human lifesigns lost" or "Hostile machines offline".
- Arenas can have wall mounts (`_wallMounts`; `CombatArena_Room` has `WallMounts/WestMount` and `EastMount` on the side walls): enemies whose `EnemyDefinition.SpawnOnWall` is set (turrets) spawn there in turn instead of at the hatches.
- Waves (developer-approved): 1: 3 scientists + 1 guard; 2: 2 guards + 1 handler + 2 scientists; 3: 2 handlers + 2 guards + 1 scientist; 4: 2 handlers + 3 guards + 2 scientists.
- Scene: `CombatArena` holds the room instance, the `GameplayCamera`, `CameraBounds` (21×29 m around room and corridor), `CreatureSpawn`, `Navigation` and the light.
- Debug: the World tab shows the arena state and has Start arena now and Skip wave (kill all). `Tools/UnityCheck/evals/gc-profile-start.cs` starts the arena with god mode on, so `unity-gc-profile.sh` profiles real combat.
- `Opening/OpeningSequence` (on `Opening/CreaturePod.prefab` in `SH_PodChamber`, at the sector spawn; developer decisions, TV-46): on a save without `WorldFlag.PlayedLine` "opening", once the creature is within 4 m of the pod it holds the pod encounter (`ArenaEncounter.Held`), freezes enemy AI, suspends the creature in the pod (`ICreatureTransit`) and spawns a Scientist at each `LabConsole` work spot; the power flickers at 3 s (sun, `event:/World/Opening/PowerFlicker`), the klaxon sounds at 5 s, the pod cracks and shakes at 15 s and bursts at 20 s (glass hides, `VFX/World/PodBurst`, sound, shake and rumble). The creature starts at `_creatureStartHealth` (80%, developer decision: the first consume shows the heal; `Health.SetFraction`). The burst `Terrify`s both scientists (cowering, no attacks) and knocks them to feedable health, then the encounter `Adopt`s them, so its clear still saves the one-time arena. Holding any button for 1 s skips to the burst. A pod encounter already cleared marks it seen. F5 World: Opening state, Replay opening, Skip opening. The pod row (`SpecimenPod`) and consoles are placeholders.
- `BioNests/BioNest` (prefab `BioNests/BioNest.prefab`, developer decisions from Progression & Economy and Core Mechanics): a marked nest site (placeholder dark disc) that Grab grows for free into a nest (placeholder smooth black pods (`NestGrown.mat`; meshes to be replaced by the developer), `NestGrow` burst and FMOD event). It's an `IHoldInteractable` (developer decisions): a tap grows a site and/or sets the nest as the checkpoint (`SetCheckpoint`: `Nests.SetRespawn`, autosave, rest burst; no heal, no menu, no arena refill); holding Grab for 0.6 s (`RestHoldDuration`) grows it if needed and rests. Resting goes through `Core.Progression.Nests.Rest` with the `RespawnPoint` child (1.8 m in front). Save id: `_id`, or `<scene>/<object name>`. `CombatArena` has `BioNest_Entry` at the south end of the corridor. Resting records the active scene (the sector root when rooms stream) as the respawn scene. `ArenaEncounter` resets to Waiting (enemies removed, doors back to their start state) on `Nests.Rested` unless it's a boss arena (`_bossArena`), which saves `WorldFlag.ClearedArena` on clear and starts open and empty from then on. Grown nests show 12 tentacles (`Tentacles` child: `Creature/Rig/TetherTentacles` through Core's `INestTentacles`, packed by its own-budget `ChainTexture` into a copy of the creature's `CreatureTentacles` effect; developer decision TV-112). Developer decisions: taut, straight strands like webbing, anchored only on near-vertical surfaces at least 1 m above the nest's floor. Each tentacle raycasts out once when shown at rising pitches (6–45°, up to 9 m, Default and Props layers) and pins its tip to the first valid hit; one with no valid anchor stays hidden. Each anchored main strand then gets 0–2 branches (seeded from the nest position) that leave it between 30% and 70% of its length, start at 70% of its width there, and anchor on any surface (floor and ceiling too) within 9 m; branches emerge once the parent reaches the branch point. Links then join random pairs of neighbouring anchored mains (25–85% along each, at half the thinner parent width, both ends riding the parents) until the nest has `_targetTotal` (32) strands; branches stop early so the total stays 32. Every strand sags 4% of its length at the middle (`_slack`). Packed by a `ChainTexture` with `_budget` 72 (developer decisions). The strand only vibrates slightly. Growing makes them shoot out over 1.6 s; resting or setting the checkpoint flares them (thicker, stronger tremor, 0.9 s). Ungrown sites pulse their disc scale. World tab: last nest, Grow all nests, Rest at nearest nest, Teleport to nearest nest. Also Flare nearest nest tentacles.
- `Shortcuts/Shortcut` (prefabs `ShortcutTunnel`, a low crawl grate that lifts, and `OneWayDoor`, a slab that sinks; design: Core Mechanics, Respawns and Progression & Economy, Traversal rules):
  - **Opening:** it opens only from the far side, where its `Lever` child stands; the lever's forward points away from the blocker. It's an `IInteractable` (`prompt.shortcut.open`) whose `CanInteract` checks `ShortcutProgress.IsFarSide` against the creature. It can also open when the nest in `_openOnNestGrown` grows.
  - **Saving:** it saves through `Core.Progression.ShortcutProgress` (`WorldFlag.OpenedShortcut`, with an autosave), plays `event:/World/Shortcut/Unlock`, and starts open when saved.
  - **Placement:** never place one inside an arena.
  - **Debug:** `Shortcuts/Debug/Resources/DebugShortcutRig.prefab` (a wall with a tunnel and a door, far side +Z). World tab: shortcuts open, Open all, Close all, Spawn shortcut test rig.
- `Security/` (design: Progression & Economy, Security system): the boss arena is sealed by a `SecurityDoor` until all three `SecurityRelay`s of its system are destroyed.
  - **Relay** (`SecurityRelay.prefab`): a destructible cabinet on the `Enemy` layer, so weapons and soft-lock hit it. It has its own `Health` (60 HP placeholder, `Bleeds` off) and a `Hurtbox`. Death saves it through `Core.Progression.SecurityRelays.Destroy(system, id)` and swaps to the `Broken` child, with the `RelaySparks` burst and the `event:/World/Security/RelayDestroyed` sound. Saved relays start broken.
  - **Door** (`SecurityDoor.prefab`): Armed until 2 of 3 relays are gone; then PoweringDown, with its warning lights flickering and the `PowerDown` sound; then it slides its slab into the floor and drops its blocker at 3/3 (`DoorOpen`). It snaps to its saved state on `Start`.
  - **Cables:** each door `Cable` (relay id, a `LineRenderer` on `TurretLaser.mat`, a lamp) goes dark when its relay dies. They are authored per placement, from the door toward each relay room.
  - **Hooks:** `SecurityRelays.RelayDestroyed(system, count)` is the hook for the PA lines and the map counter (`SecurityRelays.Count(system)`).
  - **Debug:** `Security/Debug/Resources/DebugSecurityRig.prefab` (door plus relays A, B, C of system `Debug`). World tab: security readout, Spawn security test rig, Destroy nearest relay, Reset relays.
- Enemies are instantiated per wave; pool them when a room's spawn counts make it show up in the profiler.

## Sectors (generated)

- **Sector specs** (developer decision: sectors are data): each sector is a JSON spec `Data/Sectors/<Name>.sector.json` (`Editor/SectorSpec`, read with Newtonsoft by `Editor/SectorSpecLoader` into a `SectorPlan`): sector id, root scene, folder, Addressables group, colour, spawn (cell centre) and rooms, each with `role`, `rect` [x, y, w, h] in cells, `halls` (cell paths), `doors` (`arena` for arena doors), `waves` (enemy prefab name + count) and typed `place`ments. Positions are `at` (cell centre, `RoomContext.CellCenter`), `cell` (cell corner) or `world`. Placement types: `nest`, `nestDoor` (`nest`), `terminal` (`log`), `relay` (`id`, `system` defaults to the sector), `securityDoor`, `crawl` (`id`, `opensHere`, `nest`, `otherAt`, `otherYaw`), `pickup` (`prefab` under `Pickups/`, optional `name`, `itemId`), `prefab` (asset path), `paZone` (`sequence`, `size`, `delay`), `elevator` and `transit` (a same-floor transit door, `Transit/TransitDoor.prefab`, `TravelKind.Corridor`; both take `destination` root scene, `arrivalAt` + `arrivalOffset`, `arrivalYaw`, `requiredBoss`, `requiredAbility`), `mapGate`, `hazard` (`kind` Electric/Fire/Acid/Radiation/Cryo, `size` [x, y, z] or [x, z]), `freight` (a crate station: `destination`, `arrivalAt` + `arrivalOffset`, `arrivalYaw`) and `hook` (a named one-off in `Editor/SectorHooks`; keep them rare). A new sector is a new spec file (the builder also adds its root scene to Build Settings and its registry to MapAtlas; wave enemies are found by prefab name): `SectorPlans` (`For(sector)`, `Build(sector)`, menu TentacleVania > World > Build Selected Sector Spec) finds every spec, and the sector evals and `unity-sector-sweep.sh` use it. Add a placement type to the loader when content repeats rather than a hook. `Tools/UnityCheck/evals/sector-fingerprint.cs` dumps every room scene of a registry (`fpRegistry`, `fpOut`) as sorted object/component/value lines: rebuilds are deterministic, so diffing two dumps proves a refactor kept a sector identical (scene files churn file IDs on every rebuild; restore them with `git checkout` when nothing changed).
- `Editor/SectorBuilder` builds a sector from a `SectorPlan` (`Editor/SectorPlan.cs`: rooms with grid rects, hallway paths, openings, role and waves, plus `Place` callbacks built from the spec's placements). Per room it makes a scene `<Folder>/<Scene>.unity`: a root with `Room` (`_sector`), a `Layout` child (`LevelLayout` on `SciFiTopDown`, `NavMeshSurface` on its children's physics colliders) holding the `LayoutRoom`, `LayoutHallway`s (1 cell wide) and `LayoutOpening`s, `CameraBounds` over the scene's cells + 6 m, and for arena roles an `ArenaEncounter` (waves asset `Data/Sectors/<Sector>/<Scene>_Waves.asset`, 8 inset spawn points, optional wall mounts, trigger over the room inset 1.5 m, `_bossArena` for `PlanRole.Boss`, id `<Sector>/<Scene>`). Arena openings use `Arenas/ArenaDoorCell.prefab` (3 m slab, starts open); `ArenaEncounter` collects the doors of its own scene when `_doors` is empty, and only a fighting arena claims `ArenaEncounter.Active`. Then it bakes the NavMesh synchronously (`NavMeshSurface.BuildNavMesh`) and saves it as `<Folder>/NavMesh-<Scene>.asset` (LFS by name; `LayoutGenerator.BakeNavMesh` bakes asynchronously, so a scene saved and closed right after it ships with no NavMesh and enemies can't move in builds), fills the waves asset in place and saves it (an asset edited through `SerializedObject` right after `CreateAsset` can reach disk empty), updates the sector registry (`<Folder>/<Root>Rooms.asset`, Addressables group = sector name, included in builds), copies the `StreamingTest` root as the sector root on first build (`CreatureSpawn` at the plan's spawn) and adds the registry to `MapAtlas`.
- **Auto tunnels** (developer decisions): after the registry bake, `Editor/AutoTunnels` pairs every room (with a rect, not a boss room) that is `MinRoomsFromNest` (3) or more rooms from any nest room, at most `MaxPerSector` (3, the farthest first; developer decision) (nest markers; `Shortcuts/AutoTunnelRules`, unit tested, BFS over `Neighbours`) with its nearest nest room. It places `AutoTunnel_*` Crawlspace ends (id `<sector>/Auto-<room>`, opened from the far end) on the free wall cell farthest from the room centre: not next to a door, not against another room or hallway, 3.5 m from other content. It deletes the previous auto ends on every build. Today: SH_A3, SH_A6 and SH_R2 → SH_PodChamber; BL_A3, BL_A7 and BL_R1 → BL_EntryNest.
- **Cross-scene joins:** each hallway belongs to one room scene; where a hallway or room meets a space in another scene, both scenes put a `Doorway` (or arena door) opening on the shared edge. `LayoutGrid` treats an opening on an outer edge (no space beyond it in this layout) as open, so each scene leaves its side of the join free of walls and corner pieces. Rebuilding overwrites the scenes (hand edits are lost); add content in the sector spec. Build one room with `SectorPlans.Build("SH", "SH_A1")` (close that scene first: a scene can't be overwritten while open).

## Map and fast travel

- Each `RoomRegistry` also holds the sector's map data: `_sector` (id, named by the `Dialogue` table's `sector.<id>`), `_rootScene` (what fast travel loads), `_color`, and `_markers`, baked by `RoomRegistryTools.Update` from the room scenes: `BioNest` (id `SaveId`, respawn point and yaw), `SecurityRelay` (`<system>/<id>`), `SecurityDoor`, `Map/MapGate` (place one at every ability gate; the map shows a generic blocked mark) `HoarderMerchant`, `Pickups/Pickup` (id `SaveId`), `Lore/LoreTerminal` (id the log's) and `Shortcuts/Crawlspace` (`Tunnel`, both ends share the tunnel id; `AutoTunnels` adds its own after placing them); pickups and logs show once their room is explored and hide when collected (developer decision). Rerun the update after placing any of them (sector rebuilds do).
- `Map/Resources/MapAtlas` lists every sector registry (only `StreamingTestRooms` until the slice sectors exist; add theirs). `RoomStreamer` marks the current room explored (`Core.Progression.MapProgress`, `WorldFlag.ExploredRoom`).
- `Hazards/HazardZone` (`Hazard_<Kind>.prefab`, a glowing floor slab scaled to the zone; placed by the spec's `hazard` placement): a box over the floor (`_size`, local) checked against the creature every frame; inside it, every `_tick` 0.25 s, Electric/Fire/Acid/Radiation deal `_damagePerSecond` 25 (developer rule: heavy damage over time, short stretches can be braved, long ones can't; placeholder number) as `HitFlags.Hazard | DamageOverTime` of the matching `DamageType`, unless the creature has the resistance (`HazardRules.ResistanceFor`: `ElectricResistance`, `FireResistance`, `AcidResistance`, `RadiationResistance`, passive `TraversalAbilityDefinition`s in `Abilities/Resistances/`, unlocked like boss rewards in `Build.Abilities`); Cryo vents never damage and slow to `_slow` ×0.5. Hazard gates must be long enough that they can't be crossed without the resistance (about 4 s or more of crossing). Enemies ignore hazards. F5 World: per-resistance debug toggles, Spawn <kind> hazard ahead.
- `Hazards/ArenaPylon` (`ArenaPylon.prefab`: a post with a lamp and an Electric `HazardZone` floor 6×6 m in front of it): while a live Pylon Guard is within `_guardRange` 20 m it cycles off 3 s → warning 1.2 s (the floor blinks) → on 2.5 s (the hazard deals damage); with no guard it goes dormant (lamp dark). Electric Resistance makes the creature immune. Place it with a `prefab` spec placement.
- `Hazards/FloorPattern`: Electric `HazardZone`s fired in bitmask patterns (cycled in order: gap 3 s → warning 1.2 s, the chosen zones blink → on 2.5 s) only while a live boss within `_bossRange` 40 m has left its intro (any `Enemy.IsBoss`, so World needs no Bosses reference). Used by boss arenas such as Okafor's test chamber.
- `Map/Resources/WorldGraph` (`Map/WorldGraph`) is the whole Site from the approved Site Beta Map (v2, linked from Progression & Economy): 12 sectors (id, floor L0–L3, column) and 15 connections (route kind, `Requires`: ability ids or `boss:<id>`, all needed; `WorldGraph.IsOpen`, unit tested; `GateKey` for the map). Sector ids: SH, AD, QU (L0), RD, BL, CS (L1), BA, AR, CC (L2), DC, BS, MV (L3). Sector greybox tasks build the physical transits it lists (built so far: SH ↔ AD corridor, SH ↔ RD elevator from the Warden arena, RD ↔ BL corridor). `unity-validate.sh` fails when an atlas sector is missing from the graph.
- `Map/FallRecovery` (self-created, persistent; developer decision): every 0.25 s it records the creature's last grounded spot snapped onto the NavMesh (forgotten when the creature object changes). Below `KillHeight` (-8 m; tunnel transits run at -3) it runs `FastTravel.Recover` (the crawl fade and teleport, with no label) back to that spot, with no damage. A living enemy below it warps onto the NavMesh within 6 m of its x/z, or dies if there is none. F5 World: Fall recoveries, Drop creature out of the map.
- `Map/FastTravel.Travel(nestId)` (grown nests only): `Nests.SetRespawn` to the marker, then `FastTravelRunner` (persistent) runs Covering (0.6 s unscaled) → `SceneFlow.Load(rootScene)` → Loading (until the load gate opens) → Revealing (0.8 s) → Idle, exposing `Cover` (0–1) for the UI transition, resetting the time scale to 1 and autosaving on arrival. The creature spawns at the nest through the normal respawn.
- F5 World: Map (explored rooms, markers, travel phase), Reveal sector map, Forget sector map, Grow every atlas nest. The `StreamingTest` rooms have test nests in Room 1 and Room 5 and a `MapGate` in Room 3.

## Sector transit

- `Transit/FreightCrate` (`Transit/FreightCrate.prefab`: placeholder crate with an orange band and a lamp lit once cleared; an `IInteractable`: `prompt.crate.ride`, or `prompt.crate.locked` without Freight Clearance): developer rule, each crate station follows one fixed route (`_destinationScene`, `_arrivalPosition`, `_arrivalYaw`) and the ride is a short transition, `FastTravel.Transit(..., TravelKind.Freight)` with the "FREIGHT CRATE" label (`transit.freight`) and `event:/World/FreightCrate/Ride`. ## Room streaming

- A streamed sector is a root scene (light, `CreatureSpawn`, `GameplayCamera`, `CombatHud`, volume, `RoomStreamer`) plus one scene per arena or corridor, loaded additively through Addressables (address = scene name). Room scenes are not in Build Settings. The streaming focus is `RoomStreamer.FocusOverride`, else the creature, else `CameraFocus.Target` (never the camera alone: boss intros point the camera at the boss).
- **Registry:** `Streaming/RoomRegistry` (one per sector) lists each room's scene, XZ bounds and neighbours, plus `LoadRadius` (18 m) and `UnloadRadius` (28 m; the gap keeps edge rooms from reloading). Fill it with `RoomRegistryTools.Update(registry, group, includeInBuild)` (menu TentacleVania > World > Update Selected Room Registry, group `Rooms`): it measures each scene's renderers, links rooms whose bounds touch (within 1 m), adds the scenes to the Addressables group and removes them from Build Settings. Rerun it after moving a room. Pass the sector's own Addressables group (`StreamingTest` for the test chain): a new group name moves the scenes into a new group.
- **Rules:** `Streaming/RoomStreamingRules` (pure, unit tested). The current room is the one containing the creature; it sticks while the creature stands on a shared edge. The current room and its neighbours always load; others load within `LoadRadius` and unload past `UnloadRadius`.
- **Streamer:** `RoomStreamer` (in the root scene) ticks every 0.2 s from `CameraFocus.Target`, or before the spawn from the nest respawn or its `_spawnPoint`. It holds `Core.Scenes.LoadGate` until the first rooms finish loading; `CreatureSpawner` waits for the gate, so the creature never spawns over nothing. `RoomStreamer.CurrentRoomChanged` and `CurrentScene` report the room.
- **Per room:** each room scene's root has a `Room` that enables its `CameraBounds` only while it's current, so camera bounds hand over at room boundaries. Its `_sector` id (`SH`, `BL`: the `Dialogue` table's `sector.<id>` keys) becomes `Progress.Sector` when the room is current (or on enable with no streamer), which the save slot list shows; no room sets one yet. Each room bakes its own NavMesh (`NavMeshSurface` on its `Geometry`, Children, PhysicsColliders, data next to the scene). NavMeshes of neighbouring rooms don't link, so enemies stay in their room.
- **Nests and reloads:** `BioNest` records the active scene, the sector root, as the respawn scene, so death and Continue reload the whole sector.
- **Leaving:** a `SceneFlow` scene change unloads the rooms with everything else; Addressables then warns once, "Failed to remove scene from Addressables profiler", which is harmless.
- **Test chain:** `Scenes/StreamingTest/StreamingTest.unity` with `StreamingTest_Room1`–`5` (arena, corridor, arena, corridor, arena along +Z) and `StreamingTestRooms.asset`, in the Addressables group `StreamingTest` (not included in builds). In the editor every room loads within a frame or two, so check hitches in a build.
- **Debug:** World tab "Rooms" (current room, in-flight loads, each room's state), Pause room streaming, Load all rooms, Unload distant rooms.

## Level layout tool (editor only)

Non-destructive room builder (developer decisions; the wiki's Room Dressing Tool page is the spec). Runtime data in `Layout/` (`TentacleVania.World.Layout`), editor code in `Editor/` (`TentacleVania.World.Editor`, the first area editor assembly), unit tests in `Tests/`.

- `LevelLayout` (one per room scene) holds a `Tileset`, a `DressingSet` and child shapes: `LayoutRoom` (grid `RectInt`s; overlapping or touching rects of one room merge), `LayoutHallway` (cell path with 90° bends and a width in cells) and `LayoutOpening` (cell + side + width, kind `Doorway` (an open gap) or any `Prefab` such as an arena door; developer decision: the old `Door` kind, which placed the pack's blocking door model, is gone, and the enum keeps Doorway = 0, Prefab = 2 so saved openings don't shift). Grid size is the tileset's `CellSize` (3 m for `SciFiTopDown`). The shapes are the source of truth: `Generated` is deleted and rebuilt on every change; never edit it. Hand-placed art goes under `Manual`, which the tool never touches and keeps props away from.
- `LayoutGrid` (pure, tested) owns the rules: room cells win over hallway cells; edges between different rooms are walls unless an opening joins them; a hallway edge is open where the hallway runs into the neighbouring space (so a hallway end opens into a room, but one running alongside it doesn't); hallways joining each other open the same way.
- `Tileset`: a floor slot and any number of `WallLayer`s (here Walls, Wall Tops, Columns), each with Straight, Corner (replaces both walls of a convex corner cell), ConvexPost and ConcavePost slots. Entries carry weight, offset, rotation and scale in a canonical pose (see the tileset inspector note: walls on the cell's north edge, corners north+east, posts at the north-east vertex). Pack pivots differ (this pack's floors pivot at a corner, walls at another), so calibrate entries with the tileset inspector's "Preview in scene" (a `DontSave` sample layout at 200,0,200 that rebuilds on every edit) and `unity-capture.sh`. `Spawn` keeps the prefab root's own rotation (the pack's wall tops carry 270°).
- Variants are seeded per space (`LayoutRandom`, stable across rebuilds; Reroll changes the seed); `FloorPins`/`WallPins` on a space force a variant.
- `DressingSet`: props with zones (Wall, Corner, Center, NearDoor), category (WalkThrough: colliders off; Dynamic: on the `Props` layer, which ignores Creature and Enemy; Cover: blocking, capped by `MaxCoverPerSpace`), footprint radius, weight; per-zone density and spacing; openings keep `DoorClearance` clear; room cells walled on two opposite sides get no props. Hallways (developer decision) only get Wall-zone props no larger than `HallwayMaxRadius` (0.35 m), against their walls, never corner, centre or near-door props (the sample set's small props are the pipe and the short vent). Lock selected generated props from the overlay to pin them (`PinnedProps` on their space, survive rerolls); Unlock removes the pin.
- Editing: select the layout or any shape for the Scene View "Level Layout" overlay (add Room / Hallway / Opening, Rebuild, Rebuild + bake, Reroll all, Lock/Unlock props, Manual group). Selected shapes get grid-snapped handles (room edge sliders and centre move, hallway points, opening edge); releasing a handle rebuilds when `AutoRegenerate` is on. Each room, hallway and opening draws a pickable gizmo (room rects, hallway cells, an opening marker), so clicking a shape's area in the Scene View selects it; the generator disables picking on the generated floors, walls and doors (`SceneVisibilityManager`) so those clicks reach the gizmos, while props stay pickable for Lock props. The layout gizmo draws walls (white), openings (yellow) and open joins (green). Generated tiles are marked static; Rebuild + bake rebakes the scene's `NavMeshSurface`s (`LayoutSandbox` has one, PhysicsColliders on Default).
- Assets: `Data/Layout/SciFiTopDown_Tileset.asset` and `SciFiTopDown_Dressing.asset` (third-party pack `Assets/ThirdParty/SciFiTopDown/`). Only straight walls with the 0.87 m footprint are in the tileset (Wall_02 and 19–22 are thicker); the bonus floors pivot on the opposite corner (offset +1.5, −1.5); the pack's doors are single leaves, so `Prefabs/Layout/Door_1_Pair` and `Door_2_Pair` put a left and right leaf either side of the cell centre (Door_3 is 3.9 m wide, too wide for one cell). `Panel_01` is a curved trim piece, not a floor plate. Not yet: 45° diagonals and camera-facing low walls (wiki spec).
